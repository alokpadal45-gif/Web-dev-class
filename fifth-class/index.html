Donald John Trump is an American businessman, television personality, and politician who served as the 45th President of the United States from 2017 to 2021. He is known for his unconventional leadership style and influential role in U.S. politics.

Early Life and Career

Born on June 14, 1946, in Queens, New York, Trump started his career in his family's real estate business. He expanded it into a global brand, developing skyscrapers, hotels, and golf resorts, and became a prominent public figure through the reality TV show "The Apprentice."

Presidency and Policies

Trump's presidency focused on immigration reform, tax cuts, deregulation, and America-first foreign policies. His tenure was marked by strong economic growth prior to the COVID-19 pandemic, but also by political controversies and two impeachments.

Post-Presidency

After leaving office in January 2021, Trump remains an influential figure in the Republican Party and has continued to engage in political campaigns and public commentary.
